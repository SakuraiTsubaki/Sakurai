# Japanese Bank 00 — `$0774-$09D9`

This range continues the Japanese Red V1.0/V1.1 Bank 00 reconstruction immediately after the text-command jump table.

Recovered structured source includes:

- `GetRowColAddressBgMap`, `ClearBgMap`, and `FillBgMapCommon`
- `RedrawRowOrColumn`
- automatic one-third BG transfer during VBlank
- `TransferBgRows`
- `VBlankCopyBgMap`, `VBlankCopyDouble`, and `VBlankCopy`
- overworld water/flower animation in `UpdateMovingBgTiles`
- the two 16-byte flower graphics tiles embedded at `$09AF` and `$09BF`
- `SoftReset` at `$09CF`

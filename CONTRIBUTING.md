# Contributing

Sakurai is the research-control subset of the wider repository ecosystem.

Contributions should improve research, release identity, provenance, comparison, specifications, verification, indexing, or other control-layer material.

- Identify the originating repository/project and exact target identity when relevant.
- Preserve provenance and stable identifiers.
- Separate confirmed observations from hypotheses.
- Prefer references and stable manifests over unnecessary duplicate mirrors.
- Record hashes and reproducible evidence where practical.
- Keep retail or rebuilt playable ROM images out of the repository.

Use focused commits and describe why a control or research claim is correct.

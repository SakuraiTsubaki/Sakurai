# Pokémon Crystal Expanded Pokédex — 1025 / 1351 / 1579 scope

Dataset scope frozen from PokéAPI on 2026-09-07.

## Three-layer identity model

1. **Species layer — 1025 records**
   - National Pokédex #0001–#1025.
   - Shared species metadata: National number, breeding/growth family data, capture data, evolution family identity, Pokédex seen/caught identity.

2. **Battle variety layer — 1351 records**
   - PokéAPI `pokemon` records.
   - Includes the 1025 default varieties plus 326 alternate varieties.
   - This is the authoritative Crystal BaseData layer for stats/types and other parameters that can differ by variety.
   - 32-byte Crystal-style BaseData × 1351 = 43,232 bytes, so it still fits in three 16 KiB ROM banks.
   - Proposed layout: bank $80 = variety 1–512, bank $81 = 513–1024, bank $82 = 1025–1351 (+ 185 reserved entries).

3. **Visual form layer — 1579 records**
   - PokéAPI `pokemon-form` records.
   - Maps each visual form to one of the 1351 battle varieties.
   - 1579 total form records = 228 more records than battle varieties.
   - Cosmetic forms share the same battle variety/BaseData while selecting different sprite/palette/name/form metadata.

## Runtime mapping

`FormSlot (1..1579) -> VarietySlot (1..1351) -> SpeciesID (1..1025)`

Recommended persistent mon identity:
- species_id: 16-bit National species identity
- form_slot: 16-bit compact form identity
- variety is resolved from form_slot through a ROM lookup table

This preserves fast species comparisons for scripts/evolution/breeding while allowing all visual forms to resolve to the correct battle parameters.

## Pokédex behavior

- Main National Pokédex remains 1025 numbered entries.
- Seen/Caught flags remain species-based: 1025 bits each.
- Add optional form-seen data separately so collecting a new cosmetic form does not create a fake National Dex number.
- Pokédex detail screen can expose a form selector for forms already seen.

## Graphics policy

- Keep all 1579 logical form records.
- Physical graphics are content-deduplicated: byte-identical front/back sprites, palettes, animations, or other assets share the same pointer rather than being stored repeatedly.
- Gender/cosmetic/battle-only forms therefore remain addressable without needless ROM duplication.

## Gen II adaptation

- Battle varieties get Crystal-compatible stats/types/catch/base EXP/gender/hatch/growth/egg groups/held-item mapping/evolution/learnset/TM compatibility.
- Pure cosmetic forms do not duplicate those parameters; they inherit from their mapped variety.
- Modern mechanics absent from Crystal (Abilities, Natures, Terastal, Dynamax/Gigantamax transformation logic, etc.) are not silently emulated. A form may still exist as a fixed record/visual where appropriate.

## Revision to Phase 1

The previous 1025-row expanded BaseData assumption is superseded.
The already reserved banks $80–$82 are sufficient; the table is expanded from 1025 to **1351** 32-byte battle variety records, with a separate **1579-entry form map** and a **1025-entry species map**.

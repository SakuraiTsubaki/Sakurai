# CRYSTAL v11 cutover

Status: active — 2026-09-13.

CRYSTAL now follows repository architecture v11. Canonical release coordinates are under `GEN-02/CRYSTAL/RELEASES/GBC/CART/<RELEASE-ID>/`. Legacy `SOURCE`, `TARGET`, `COMPARE`, and game-local `REFERENCE` ownership roots are migration-only and receive no new work.

Tsubaki is the complete non-ROM superset. Every non-ROM artifact generated here is mirrored to Tsubaki; Sakurai retains the research/control subset. ROM binaries are not committed.

Verified source set: BXTJ-HV0, BYTE-HV0, BYTE-HV1, BYTD-HV0, BYTS-HV0, BYTF-HV0, BYTI-HV0.

# Silver path migration to Architecture V11

Canonical Silver axes are `RELEASES`, `COMPARES`, `PROJECTS`, `KNOWLEDGE`, and `VERIFY`.

- `COMPARE/` -> `COMPARES/`
- `TARGET/SILVER-MODERNIZATION/` -> `PROJECTS/SILVER-MODERNIZATION/`
- `SOURCE/` -> release-centric `RELEASES/` metadata; the complete pre-V11 tree is preserved at `KNOWLEDGE/ROM-FORENSICS-V10/`.

ROM images and patched ROM images remain excluded. Tsubaki is the complete non-ROM superset; Sakurai is the research, analysis, comparison, observation, and verification subset.

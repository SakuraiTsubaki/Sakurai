# Asset Workflow

Sakurai keeps assets when they are part of curated research, comparison, provenance, verification, localization, or control evidence.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the exact semantic coordinate and owner path.
2. Record source/provenance before transformation.
3. Extract or reconstruct reproducibly.
4. Keep human-viewable previews such as PNGs when they materially support review.
5. Compute hashes when identity/deduplication matters.
6. Deduplicate only after byte/hash identity is verified.
7. Record shared relationships in metadata/manifests.
8. Verify the result and update its evidence level.
9. Prefer small, reviewable commits.

Sakurai does not need to mirror every intermediate artifact; Tsubaki is the complete eligible non-ROM archive/production superset.

# Research Guide

Every record should identify the originating repository/project and exact target whenever differences may matter: game, release, build, revision, region, language, bank/section, address, archive/member, symbol, or artifact ID.

Confidence levels: **Hypothesis**, **Observed**, **Reproduced**, and **Matched**.

Preserve commits, hashes, commands, tools, offsets, symbols, manifests, public references, and other evidence needed to reproduce important cross-repository findings.

# Research Guide

## Evidence first

Every material claim should be traceable to evidence: a verified target, public source, official material, repository history, technical documentation, reproducible extraction, comparison output, hash, or other attributable source.

## Confidence

Keep these concepts distinct:

- hypothesis
- observed fact
- reproduced result
- exact match

Do not upgrade confidence because an interpretation is plausible or visually similar.

## Scope

Always record the exact generation/game/release/revision/language/platform/dump scope when it matters. A finding in one target must not silently become a universal claim.

## Provenance

When practical record source URL/repository and revision, file/path, symbol/address/offset/range, tool and command, hashes, and notes about uncertainty.

## Reproducibility

Prefer scripts, manifests, tables, and commands that let another researcher reproduce a result. Keep generated outputs when they materially support verification and belong to Sakurai's curated control layer.

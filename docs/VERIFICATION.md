# Verification

Use the following repository-wide evidence levels.

## Unverified
A proposed identity, interpretation, mapping, or relationship that has not yet been independently checked.

## Observed
Directly supported by a target, source, extracted record, repository artifact, or other attributable evidence.

## Reproduced
The result can be recreated with documented steps, tooling, inputs/coordinates, and expected outputs.

## Matched
The declared scope exactly matches its expected identity, bytes, hash, structure, or behavior according to the documented verification method.

## Evidence to record

Depending on scope, include hashes, semantic coordinates, offsets/ranges, symbols, tool versions, commands, logs, comparison tables, manifests, screenshots/previews, and limitations.

Do not use `Matched` for visual similarity or behavioral similarity alone when exact byte/hash identity is the claim.

# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Canonical identity
- Maintain one live owner path per project/artifact family.
- Keep semantic IDs stable and explicit.
- Remove ambiguous or catch-all identifiers instead of accumulating them.

## Phase 2 — Coverage and provenance
- Expand verified release/dump inventories as evidence becomes available.
- Record source provenance, citations, hashes, and revision relationships.
- Keep unknown values explicit rather than inferred.

## Phase 3 — Research normalization
- Normalize comparison tables, schemas, reports, and research tooling.
- Separate confirmed findings from hypotheses.
- Preserve target-specific differences.

## Phase 4 — Verification
- Add reproducible verification commands and evidence.
- Promote records from Unverified → Observed → Reproduced → Matched only with support.

## Phase 5 — Pair synchronization
- Keep Sakurai's curated subset aligned with Tsubaki's complete eligible non-ROM superset.
- Use identical semantic coordinates for shared material.

## Phase 6 — Documentation stability
- Keep repository-wide standards concise and current.
- Do not create empty or historical directory trees merely for appearance.
